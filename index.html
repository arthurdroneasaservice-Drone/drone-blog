MULTISPECTRAL TERRAIN MAPPING

Collect and analyze insightful geospatial data for projects including land development, agriculture, energy and utilities, and more.

WHAT IS MULTISPECTRAL TERRAIN MAPPING?

Multispectral terrain mapping uses specialized sensors to capture data across multiple bands of the electromagnetic spectrum, including those beyond visible light, to create detailed maps of physical environments and objects. Drone RGB, Near-infrared (NIR), and short-wave infrared (SWIR) cameras can capture data on any terrain and create a 3D point cloud to provide deep insights.

APPLICABLE ACROSS INDUSTRIES

  • Land surveys
  • Detection of underground water sources
  • Vegetation indices for crop health
  • Volumetrics
  • Mineral and material identification
  • Environmental monitoring
  • Excavations
  • Renewable energy operations

TRANSFORMATIVE AGRICULTURAL INSIGHTS

Multispectral sensors map agricultural terrain to assess crop health, soil moisture, weeds, and plant growth. They can also penetrate vegetation to analyze orchards and undeveloped land.

PRECISION DATA, QUICKLY

Drone as a Service uses multispectral drone data to create high-density 3D point clouds for accurate mapping, design, and planning. This enables fast, cost-effective data collection for project tracking and decision-making.
